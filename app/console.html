What is real and what is still missing

Everything on the map comes from public sources. Gaps are listed so nobody mistakes a prototype for a finished tool.

Status of each layer

LoadedParcels. 3,125 parcels in the study area from the PASDA Centre County parcel layer. Condo units are merged into one lot.
LoadedToday's districts. The Borough's zoning layer, assigned to each parcel by largest overlap. Lots on a district boundary can be wrong.
TracedDraft districts. The Borough publishes the draft only as a PDF map dated 6/15/2026. District shapes were pulled from that PDF and lined up with the Borough boundary. The alignment check (0.9965 overlap) covers the PDF fit only, not each district's shape. These are a close read of the PDF, not official data and not the Borough's GIS.
LoadedDraft heights, uses and parking. From the draft's incentive schedule, use tables and Part E parking table (second draft, June 2026). The draft can change.
PartialToday's rules. Read from ecode360 for 13 districts, with section numbers. Overlays are not applied, and several districts, including the University Planned District, have no rule card yet.
RuleQuestion answers. "You may" means the draft use table lists the use as permitted. "You must ask first" means conditional use or special exception, which needs approval before building. "You may not" means the draft does not list it as allowed. Other rules (setbacks, lot coverage, density, overlays) also apply and are not applied here. Today's uses are not loaded as a table, so question answers cover the draft only.
FlagNext to a district line. A lot that touches a lot in a different draft district gets a warning, because the draft shapes were traced from a PDF and the lot may belong to the neighbor.
PartialStreet addresses. Joined from the Borough's tax parcel layer, so 3,088 of 3,125 parcels have one. Parcels outside the Borough have none.
PartialBuildings. Footprints come from Microsoft's 2019 US building dataset, so recent demolition or construction is missing.
MissingBuilding heights. Existing buildings are drawn low. Existing-versus-allowed comparisons are footprint only.
MissingSetbacks, lot coverage, density, parking limits. The block shows only the height limit over the whole lot. A real building would be smaller.
